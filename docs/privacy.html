What Clips_GTA processes

Clips_GTA is a desktop application. Gameplay files, clips, and temporary media stay on your computer. We do not run a cloud server that collects your VODs.

TikTok

If you use Connect TikTok, TikTok identifies you and issues an access token so Clips_GTA can upload clips you export. That token is stored only on your machine. Clips_GTA does not sell personal data or use your videos for ads.

YouTube or browser cookies

If you allow Clips_GTA to use browser cookies to download a live or VOD, they are used only for that download on your device.

Third parties

YouTube, Twitch, and TikTok have their own policies when you visit or upload there.
