1. Who we are

CLIPS is desktop software and this public product website. The operator of CLIPS is the developer of the open repository https://github.com/DannyElian/clips-app (referred to in this policy as the CLIPS developer). For privacy requests, use https://github.com/DannyElian/clips-app/issues with the subject “CLIPS privacy”.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, Inc. provides hosting infrastructure. GitHub’s own privacy policy applies to requests made to github.com and github.io.

2. Scope

This policy covers:

  • the CLIPS website at https://dannyelian.github.io/clips-app/ and its pages (Home, Download, Support, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service);
  • the CLIPS desktop application you run on your own computer;
  • optional connection to TikTok so you can upload clips you exported.

It does not cover TikTok, YouTube, Twitch, GitHub, or other sites you visit outside CLIPS. Those services have their own policies.

3. Information we process

3.1 Information this website collects

The CLIPS website is a static product site. We do not operate an account system, login form, newsletter, or advertising pixel on this site. We do not ask you to create a CLIPS cloud account.

The host (GitHub Pages) may automatically process technical logs such as IP address, browser type, date/time, and the URL requested, as described in GitHub’s documentation. CLIPS does not run its own analytics database on this website.

3.2 Information the desktop app processes on your device

When you run CLIPS locally, the application may process:

  • Video and audio files you select or download, of any type (comedy, stream, gameplay, IRL, or other).
  • Source URLs you paste (for example YouTube or Twitch links).
  • Job settings (clip length, count, quality, layout, crop).
  • Temporary files (downloads, audio extracts, face-detection frames, exported MP4s) in folders on your disk.
  • Optional browser cookies from a browser you choose, used only to authenticate a download on your machine.
  • Optional speech-to-text output generated on-device to suggest captions. That text stays in the local job folder unless you copy it elsewhere.

CLIPS is designed so that this media is not uploaded to a CLIPS cloud backend, because CLIPS does not operate one.

3.3 Information from TikTok if you connect an account

Only if you click connect TikTok inside the desktop app:

  • OAuth tokens issued by TikTok (access token, refresh token, expiry);
  • basic profile fields TikTok returns for the authorized scopes (for example open_id and display name) so the app can show who is connected;
  • identifiers TikTok returns after an upload (publish id / inbox status).

Those values are stored in a local session file on your computer. We do not receive a copy on a CLIPS server.

4. TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting

CLIPS uses TikTok Login Kit (desktop) and the TikTok Content Posting API so you can authorize CLIPS to upload a video file you exported to your own TikTok account.

Scopes requested (as configured in the TikTok developer app named CLIPS):

  • user.info.basic — show the connected TikTok display name in the CLIPS interface;
  • video.publish and/or video.upload — upload an MP4 you exported, as permitted by TikTok for the app’s audit status.

Redirect URI used by the desktop app: http://localhost:8787/api/tiktok/callback. That address exists only on the computer running CLIPS.

TikTok is operated by TikTok Technology Limited / ByteDance entities. When you sign in or a video is uploaded, TikTok processes that data under TikTok’s Privacy Policy and TikTok’s Terms of Service. CLIPS does not sell TikTok data, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with unrelated advertisers.

You can disconnect TikTok from the CLIPS interface (logout), which deletes the local token file. You should also revoke CLIPS in TikTok’s in-app or web connected-apps settings if you want TikTok to stop honoring the grant.

5. Local processing

Highlight detection, encoding, optional face detection, and optional transcription run on your computer. You control deletion by deleting the CLIPS project folder, the local data/ directory, or individual job folders.

6. Third-party services

Depending on how you use CLIPS, third parties may process data:

  • GitHub Pages — hosting this website.
  • TikTok — login and video upload if you connect an account.
  • YouTube, Twitch, or other sites whose URLs you paste — they receive the request your machine makes (subject to their terms, which you must have the right to use).
  • yt-dlp and ffmpeg — local tools; they are not CLIPS cloud vendors.

We do not sell personal information as defined under the CCPA/CPRA.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not set a first-party analytics cookie. GitHub Pages and your browser may still store standard technical data. The desktop app does not drop advertising cookies. Optional “cookies from browser” are read from a browser you already use, only to fetch a video you requested, and are not sent to CLIPS.

8. Purposes and legal bases

Where GDPR or similar law applies, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract / requested service: running the app you installed, exporting clips, publishing when you ask.
  • Legitimate interests: securing the software, answering support issues you open, maintaining this website.
  • Consent: TikTok OAuth (you start connect, you can revoke); optional cookie-based downloads.
  • Legal obligation: if we must keep or disclose information to comply with law (we do not currently operate a user database).

9. Sharing

We share information only:

  • with you, on your device;
  • with TikTok when you authorize an upload;
  • with infrastructure providers of this static website (GitHub);
  • if required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and integrity;
  • with a successor if CLIPS is transferred, with notice on this site.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

10. Retention

  • Website logs: according to GitHub Pages / GitHub practice.
  • Local media and job files: until you delete them.
  • TikTok tokens: until you log out, they expire, or you delete the local session file.
  • Support issues you file on GitHub: according to GitHub’s retention of issues.

11. Security

CLIPS keeps TikTok secrets and tokens on the local machine and does not publish them on this website. You are responsible for the physical and account security of the computer that runs CLIPS, including who can read the local .env and session files. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

12. International processing

GitHub and TikTok may process data in the United States and other countries. If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, those transfers follow the mechanisms those providers publish (for example Standard Contractual Clauses). Local CLIPS files do not leave your machine unless you copy them or you publish to TikTok.

13. Children

CLIPS is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your country, which may be 16+). Do not use CLIPS or connect TikTok if you do not meet TikTok’s age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete local guidance and any GitHub issue content we control.

14. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, to portability, and to withdraw consent. Because CLIPS does not host your videos, the practical way to delete media is to delete it on your computer and, if needed, delete videos on TikTok. For requests about this website or GitHub issues, use the contact section. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

15. California and similar US state laws

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in the CCPA/CPRA. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes other than those permitted. To request access or deletion of information we actually hold (for example a support issue you opened), contact us as below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

16. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change. The current version will always be published at https://dannyelian.github.io/clips-app/privacy.html. Material changes will also be noted on the CLIPS homepage when practical.
