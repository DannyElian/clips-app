Clips_GTA

Clips_GTA is a finished desktop product for creators. It cuts gameplay VODs and live recordings into vertical 9:16 clips, then publishes them to the creator’s own TikTok account with Login Kit and the Content Posting API.

Official website and Desktop URL: https://dannyelian.github.io/clips-app/

One video or two stacked

Import a single VOD or two sources (facecam + gameplay). Clips_GTA stacks them for TikTok (camera on top, game below) or keeps a full-frame layout.

Highlights or even splits

Pick best moments from the whole file, or split it into a fixed number of clips with the duration you choose. Export at 360p, 720p, or 1080p.

Post to TikTok from the app

Sign in with TikTok, then publish each exported clip. Videos are processed on your computer. Clips_GTA does not host other people’s gameplay in the cloud.

Lives and VODs

Paste a YouTube or Twitch link, or drop a local file. For lives, export the last N minutes from the DVR window instead of waiting on the live edge.

How Clips_GTA works

  1. Open Clips_GTA on your desktop (the app UI at your local Clips_GTA server).
  2. Add a gameplay file or URL. Optionally add a second camera/game video.
  3. Choose clip length, count, quality, and highlight vs split mode.
  4. Clips_GTA analyzes audio and scenes, then exports vertical MP4 files.
  5. Connect TikTok and tap TikTok on a clip to upload to your account.
