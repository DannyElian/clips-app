Face over the scene, full frame, or two videos stacked. Optional face detection places the camera pane.

Pick the moments

Best moments from energy and cuts, or even splits. 360p, 720p, or 1080p. Length and count are yours.

Publish when you want

Connect TikTok inside CLIPS and upload a clip you exported. You stay the publisher.

Your files stay local

Processing runs on your computer. This site does not host your videos.

How it works

  1. Open CLIPS on your computer.
  2. Drop a file or paste a link. Optionally add a second video.
  3. Choose duration, how many clips, and quality.
  4. Export, copy the caption, and post.
